Questions, answered

Straightforward answers for first-time visitors.

What is Available Margin?

Available Margin is an estimate of your financial room based on your projected cash outlook over the next 90 days. SimplBudget considers information from your connected accounts and the financial patterns it has identified to help show how much room you may have before cash gets tight. It is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Is this a budgeting app?

No. SimplBudget is a financial forecasting and decision-support app. It helps you see where your money is headed and understand what you can afford — without assigning every purchase to a category.

How do bank connections work?

Connections use Plaid. You authenticate with your institution through Plaid’s secure flow. SimplBudget does not see or store your bank username or password.

How current is my financial information?

Financial information from connected institutions may take time to update, so SimplBudget's forecasts are estimates based on the information currently available.

When will it be available?

SimplBudget is coming soon on iPhone. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you when access opens.

Is this financial advice?

No. SimplBudget provides informational estimates only. Always review your own finances before making spending decisions.
